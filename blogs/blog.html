Color Mode, RGB vs. CMYK: A Quick Comparison

Feature RGB (Red, Green, Blue) CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black)
Primary Use Digital displays (Websites, apps, video, social media) Physical printing (Business cards, flyers, apparel, packaging)
Color Creation Additive: Mixes light starting from black Subtractive: Mixes physical ink starting from a white background
Color Gamut Much larger; can display bright, vibrant, and neon colors Smaller; some bright RGB colors cannot be printed and appear muted
Why it's used Screens naturally emit light in these exact three primary colors Printers require these physical inks layered on paper to absorb/reflect light
